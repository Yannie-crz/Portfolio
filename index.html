1. Problem Background & Beneficiary Needs

Mobile street food vendors in local community settings frequently encounter discrepancies between morning commissary stock allocations and evening cash collections. Without real-time geospatial location awareness, commissary managers cannot verify cart placements, detect stock depletion during lunch hours, or prevent spoilage of unsold perishables.

2. Technical System Architecture

  • Vendor Client Layer: Responsive interface reporting GPS coordinates at set intervals to log operational cart locations.
  • Commissary Central Backend: Node.js and PHP server logic managing inventory batches, wholesale discounts, and daily return audits.
  • Relational DB Tier: Normalized MySQL schema with tables for tbl_carts, tbl_inventory_batches, tbl_daily_returns, and tbl_sales_logs.

3. Verified Results & Practical Impact

  • Eliminated manual paper log discrepancies during end-of-day cash and stock turn-ins.
  • Delivered verified interactive map visibility for all active mobile vendors across campus and commercial hub locations.
  • Successfully presented, evaluated, and approved before the CHMSU College of Computer Studies faculty panel.

Payment Disbursement System

Enterprise Requisition & Audit Architecture (SYANDE)

Workflow Architecture

The Payment Disbursement System was designed to enforce internal financial controls for institutional requisitions. It prevents unauthorized payments through a sequential 4-stage authorization pipeline.

Authorization Pipeline

  • Stage 1 - Requisition: Department staff enters payee details, fund allocation, and expense line items.
  • Stage 2 - Verification: Accounting auditor checks fund availability and supporting receipts.
  • Stage 3 - Approval: Designated executive officer authorizes payment release.
  • Stage 4 - Check Disbursement: Cashier records check disbursement number and logs final release timestamp.

Disbursement System — Audit Specifications

Security, Non-Repudiation & Relational Schema
